Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026
Core Privacy Guarantee: FilterPoint is designed with privacy-first architecture. Your clinical health profiles, kidney disease stages, custom macro limits, and food logs are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit, sell, rent, or monetize your personal health information.

1. Information We Collect and Process

FilterPoint minimizes data collection by keeping sensitive operations client-side:

  • Local Health Data: Information regarding your dietary preferences, Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) stages, diabetes parameters, or custom avoidance lists is saved exclusively in sandboxed local storage on your device.
  • Camera & Image Data: When you scan a packaged food label or fresh produce, images captured by your camera are processed in temporary memory for optical character recognition (OCR) and visual recognition. FilterPoint does not build an identification profile linked to your identity from these images.

2. Third-Party Services & Technical Processing

To provide accurate nutritional analysis and subscription processing, FilterPoint interfaces with the following service providers:

  • Google Play In-App Billing: Subscription transactions are handled directly by Google Play. We do not store or process your credit card numbers or financial account details.
  • Google Gemini Vision API: Fresh produce recognition queries may be transmitted securely to Google cloud endpoints for visual analysis. These queries contain only image data of the grocery item and carry no personal user identifiers.
  • Open Food Facts API: Barcode lookups query public nutritional databases via anonymous requests without transmitting personal health records.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your health data is stored locally on your mobile device:

  • You can permanently erase all health preferences, custom macros, and saved recipes at any time by clearing the app data in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the application.
  • Since we do not store your medical logs on remote servers, no remote deletion request is required to remove your personal health information.

4. Children's Privacy

FilterPoint is designed for adult dietary self-management and is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published directly on this page with an updated revision date.
