Frequently Asked Questions

Medical Disclaimer & Clinical Accuracy

Q: Is FilterPoint a substitute for a doctor or registered dietitian?
No. FilterPoint is a clinical nutrition shield and educational tool designed to assist with dietary tracking and recipe adjustments. Always consult your healthcare provider or renal dietitian regarding your specific medical nutritional needs.

Supported Conditions & Diets

Q: Which health conditions does FilterPoint evaluate?
FilterPoint currently supports CKD Stages 1–3, CKD Stages 4–5, Dialysis, Type 2 Diabetes, GLP-1 support, Celiac disease, Autoimmune Protocol (AIP), and custom user watchlists.

Scanning & Accuracy

Q: Why did my label scan say “Incomplete Scan” or “Non-food item detected”?
Ensure you are photographing a clear Nutrition Facts panel or raw produce item in good lighting. If the image is blurry, angled too sharply, or contains non-food objects, our built-in safety guardrails will reject it to prevent inaccurate clinical reads.
Q: Where do barcode details come from?
Barcodes are cross-referenced directly with Open Food Facts and global USDA databases through secure, anonymous lookups.

Privacy & Data Storage

Q: Where is my health profile and custom watchlist stored?
Your profile settings, slider overrides, and custom watchlists are stored locally on your device in secure app-scoped XML and SharedPreferences files. Your health data stays private and is never uploaded, tracked, or sold.

Subscriptions & Pro Features

Q: What features require a Pro subscription?
Pro access unlocks advanced AI recipe adjusting, chemically sound clinical ingredient substitutions, and extended scan history. Subscriptions are managed directly and securely through Google Play Billing.
